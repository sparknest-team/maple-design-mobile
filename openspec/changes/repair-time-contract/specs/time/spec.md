## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Explicit temporal public contracts
Time fields and panels SHALL document all actual public properties and their distinct event/slot roles.

#### Scenario: Business panel events
- **WHEN** a consumer uses public panel event types
- **THEN** select/change/confirm/update model notifications are available
- **AND** internal draft-initialize is not advertised as a business event.

#### Scenario: Panel customization
- **WHEN** actions/extra are supplied
- **THEN** their documented scopes are typed and forwarded
- **AND** field beforeCommit remains a separate payload/done/context save protocol.
